A QUICK GUIDE

Support, without the guesswork.

A “request” is simply a message asking your IT team for help.

How do I get access?

Create an account with your work email and confirm the email if asked. Your company’s IT contact then links your account to your employee record. If you have already done this, just sign in.

What do the request statuses mean?

Received: your request was saved. In progress: IT is working on it. On hold: IT is waiting for more information or another step. Resolved: IT marked the issue as fixed. Closed: the request is finished. Read the conversation for the details.

Where will I see updates?

Open My requests, click Check for updates, then open your request. Messages from IT appear in the conversation. The portal does not send automatic email updates.

Who can see my request?

You can see requests submitted through your employee account for the selected company. Authorized IT staff can handle them. You cannot see other employees’ requests or IT’s internal work notes.

What if the problem comes back?

If the request is still in progress, send a reply in its conversation. If it is resolved or closed, send a new request and include the previous request reference.
